Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Fyndable alongside Yoast or Rank Math?

Yes, but we recommend using one plugin as your primary SEO tool. Duplicate sitemaps, schemas and redirects can cause conflicts.

Why don't I see certain menu items?

Check if your license is active and if your tier grants access to that menu. Trial and DEV tiers show all menus.

How often should I run a Site Audit?

At least once a month, or immediately after major changes (new site, migration, large content release).

How do I force a Google crawl?

Use Direct Index (Google Indexing API) for important URLs. Note that the quota is limited.

My AI tools don't generate output, what now?

Check your license quota, the SaaS dashboard connection, and whether the correct API key is configured.

Does Fyndable work with the classic editor?

The Editor Assistant only works in Gutenberg. The SEO meta box works in both Gutenberg and the classic editor.

How do I update white-label settings?

Go to Settings → White Label, adjust the settings, log out and back in to see everything.

Why does GSC data differ from the Fyndable dashboards?

Dashboards cache data and may use different aggregations. Use the numbers for trends, not for exact comparisons.

Can I completely disable the dashboard?

Yes, via Settings → Advanced you can disable the full-page dashboard shell. The menus will still appear in the standard WordPress admin.

Who can I contact if I really can't figure it out?

Create a ticket via Support in the Fyndable menu or contact us through the Fyndable support channel.
