Advanced Backlinks

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Advanced backlink analysis: broken backlinks, competitor backlink targets, anchor text scoring and link opportunities.

How to use it

  1. Go to Advanced Backlinks.
  2. Enter a competitor or your own domain.
  3. Review the opportunities list.
  4. Export a prospect list and start outreach.

Example

You see that competitor.com has a link from an industry blog where you could also guest post. You note the contact and send a pitch.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Opportunities are too generic: filter by domain authority and relevance.
  • Outreach yields nothing: personalize your email and offer valuable content.
