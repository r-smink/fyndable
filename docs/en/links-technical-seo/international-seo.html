International SEO

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Additional tools for international SEO: country-specific keyword research, geo-SERP analysis, currency/language optimization and international link suggestions.

How to use it

  1. Go to AI Tools → International SEO.
  2. Choose a target country and language.
  3. Enter a keyword.
  4. Review search volume, competition and local recommendations.

Example

You sell in the Netherlands and Belgium. You analyze "solar panels" for nl-be and discover that Belgians more often search for "solar panel subsidy Flanders".

Potential issues & solutions

  • No local data: ensure SE Ranking or Ahrefs is connected for reliable volume.
