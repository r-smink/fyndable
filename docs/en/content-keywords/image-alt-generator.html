Image Alt Generator

Priority: medium | Starter+

What it does and what it gives you

Generates descriptive alt texts for images. Good for accessibility and image SEO.

How to use it

  1. Open the media library or an image in the editor.
  2. Expand the Alt Text accordion.
  3. Click Generate Alt Text.
  4. Edit the text to be descriptive and concise.

Example

Image: photo of a red bike in a city park. Fyndable returns "red city bike parked in park". Adjust to "red city bike parked in Central Park".

Potential issues & solutions

  • Alt text is too generic: add context like location or product name.
  • Keyword stuffing in alt text: keep it natural; alt is primarily for accessibility.
