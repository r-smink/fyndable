Keyword Explorer

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Expands a seed keyword with SERP title analysis and groups related terms by Jaccard similarity. Useful for discovering new content angles.

How to use it

  1. Go to AI Tools → Keyword Explorer.
  2. Enter a broad keyword, e.g. "plants".
  3. Click Explore.
  4. Review clusters like "houseplants", "plant care", "toxic plants".

Example

Seed: "bikes". Clusters that appear: "electric bikes", "road bikes", "bike maintenance". You choose "bike maintenance" for a blog series.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Results seem random: use a more specific seed or combine with Keyword Difficulty.
