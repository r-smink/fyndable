Bulk Optimizer

Priority: medium | Business+

What it does and what it gives you

Scans your entire site for missing SEO titles, meta descriptions, Open Graph tags and focus keyphrases. You can then have AI-generated meta data filled in per post or in bulk.

How to use it

  1. Go to Bulk Optimizer.
  2. Click Scan Site.
  3. Wait for the scan to analyze all published posts.
  4. Review the summary: how many posts are missing title, description, OG or keyphrase.
  5. Filter on a specific issue (e.g. Missing Title).
  6. Check the fields you want to generate.
  7. Select one or more posts and click Generate for Selected.
  8. Randomly check some results afterward.

Example

You have 80 blog posts with no SEO titles. You filter on "Missing Title", select all 80 posts, check SEO Title and Meta Description and start bulk generation.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Generation stops halfway: the process handles one post at a time to save quota. Reload and restart; already processed posts are preserved.
  • Output is not relevant: check if the focus keyphrase is correct or adjust the post content.
