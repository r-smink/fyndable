Content Calendar

Priority: medium | All tiers

What it does and what it gives you

A visual calendar where you plan posts, assign them to team members and spot content gaps.

How to use it

  1. Go to Content Calendar.
  2. Click on a date to add a new idea or post.
  3. Drag an existing post to another date to reschedule.
  4. Open the Workflow accordion in the editor to set status and assigned author.

Example

You plan three articles for next month: a pillar on the 1st, two clusters on the 8th and 15th. You assign them to your content manager.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Calendar is empty: ensure at least one post or idea exists.
  • Workflow fields not visible: these are only available with Business+ tier.
