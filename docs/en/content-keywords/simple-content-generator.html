Simple Content Generator

Priority: low-medium | All tiers

What it does and what it gives you

Quick AI generation directly in the editor for a paragraph, intro or CTA. You provide context and a word limit.

How to use it

  1. Open the AI Content accordion in the editor.
  2. Describe what you need in the field.
  3. Set the desired word count.
  4. Click Generate.
  5. Paste the result into your content.

Example

You're writing a page about "work methods" and ask: "write an 80-word intro about scrum". The AI delivers a short paragraph.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Output is too long or too short: adjust the word limit in the panel.
