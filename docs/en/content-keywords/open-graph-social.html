Open Graph & Social Sharing

Priority: medium | All tiers

What it does and what it gives you

Controls how your page looks when someone shares the link on Facebook, LinkedIn or Twitter/X (Open Graph). Social Sharing optionally adds share buttons to your page.

How to use it

  1. Open the Social Preview accordion in the editor.
  2. Adjust the Open Graph title, description and image.
  3. Save the post.
  4. Test via the Facebook Sharing Debugger or LinkedIn Post Inspector.

Example

For your homepage you use an OG title "Fyndable - Smart SEO for growing businesses" and an OG image with your logo.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Social preview shows old image: platforms cache OG data. Use their debugger to refresh.
  • Image is too large or too small: use 1200×630 px images for best display.
