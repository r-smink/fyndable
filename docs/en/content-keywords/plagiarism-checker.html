Plagiarism Checker

Priority: low | Agency

What it does and what it gives you

Checks if text may be copied or strongly resembles AI-generated content. Gives an originality score.

How to use it

  1. Open the Originality accordion in the editor.
  2. Click Check Originality.
  3. Review the score and flagged segments.
  4. Rewrite questionable passages.

Example

You run an AI text and see a score of 45%. The tool flags paragraphs that appear frequently on other sites. Rewrite those in your own words.

Potential issues & solutions

  • AI-generated content flagged as "unoriginal": use the rewriter to make the text more human.
