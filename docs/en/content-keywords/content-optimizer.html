Content Optimizer

Priority: high | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Analyzes the top 10 in Google for a keyword and builds a topic model. Then scores your text (0-100) against that model. You see exactly which terms you're missing, overusing or using well.

How to use it

  1. Go to AI Tools → Content Optimizer (or open the panel in the editor).
  2. Enter your focus keyword.
  3. Click Analyze. Fyndable fetches SERP data via the SaaS proxy.
  4. Review the list of missing, overused and used terms.
  5. Update your text.
  6. Click Re-analyze to improve your score.

Example

You target "houseplants". The model shows that competitors mention "air purifying plants", "care" and "light requirements", but you only mention "green in the house". Add those topics.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Analyze gives no result: check if your license is active and the SaaS dashboard URL is reachable.
  • Score doesn't improve: you're using missing terms too little or too forced; write natural paragraphs around those terms.
