AI Content Writer

Priority: medium | Business+

What it does and what it gives you

Writes complete articles based on a keyword. You choose type (blog, how-to, review), tone, length and structure. Fyndable automatically adds relevant internal links.

How to use it

  1. Go to AI Tools → Content Writer.
  2. Enter a keyword / topic.
  3. Choose article type, tone (formal, friendly, business) and desired word count.
  4. Click Generate.
  5. Check the draft for facts, readability and brand voice.
  6. Publish or schedule.

Example

You request a 1,200-word article in friendly tone about "climate-neutral business". Fyndable generates a draft with H2s and internal links to your sustainability page.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Output is too generic: add extra instructions, e.g. "specifically mention our service X".
  • Incorrect facts: AI can hallucinate. Verify all numbers, dates and claims.
