Content Brief

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Generates a full SEO brief based on top results: structure, headings, questions, entities, average length and internal link opportunities. Useful for writing yourself or handing off.

How to use it

  1. Go to AI Tools → Content Brief.
  2. Enter the target keyword.
  3. Click Generate Brief.
  4. Read the brief: H1, H2s, required word count and PAA questions.
  5. Use the brief as a guide in the editor.

Example

For "sustainable travel" the brief says: target 1,800 words, cover train travel, CO2 compensation, slow travel and answer "What is sustainable travel?".

Potential issues & solutions

  • Brief is too broad: specify your keyword, e.g. "sustainable travel by train".
  • AI fetches old SERP data: data is cached for 7 days; click Refresh if in doubt.
