Content Rewriter

Priority: medium | Starter+

What it does and what it gives you

Rewrites existing text. You choose a mode: SEO optimization, readability, expand, shorten, paraphrase or change tone.

How to use it

  1. Open a post or go to AI Tools → Content Rewriter.
  2. Select the text you want to rewrite.
  3. Choose the mode.
  4. Click Rewrite.
  5. Paste the improved text back.

Example

You have a paragraph about "our services" that's poorly written. You choose "Improve readability"; the AI makes sentences shorter and more active.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Meaning has changed: always compare the original and rewritten version.
  • Output too short: choose "Expand" instead of "Rewrite".
