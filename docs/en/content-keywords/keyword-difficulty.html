Keyword Difficulty

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Calculates your chance of ranking for a keyword, based on your own site's authority, existing content and internal link structure.

How to use it

  1. Go to AI Tools → Keyword Difficulty.
  2. Enter a keyword.
  3. Review the personalized score and advice.
  4. If the score is low, focus on long-tail variants first.

Example

"Health insurance" has a difficulty of 85 for your small site. The advice is to target "health insurance comparison self-employed" first.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Score is always high: build authority by scoring mid-tier keywords first.
