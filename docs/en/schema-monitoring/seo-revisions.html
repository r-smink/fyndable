SEO Revisions

Priority: low | Agency

What it does and what it gives you

Stores snapshots of SEO changes (titles, meta, schema, canonical). You can revert to a previous version or compare changes.

How to use it

  1. Open a post.
  2. Edit SEO fields and save.
  3. Go to SEO Revisions.
  4. Click a date to see the old values.
  5. Click Restore if you want to revert.

Example

You change a title and see a decline after two weeks. You restore the old title via SEO Revisions.

Potential issues & solutions

  • No revisions visible: the feature must be enabled in Settings → SEO Revisions.
  • Restore doesn't work: some fields require an extra manual check after restore.
