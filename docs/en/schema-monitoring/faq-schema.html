FAQ Schema

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Marks questions and answers so Google can show an FAQ accordion in the SERP.

How to use it

  1. Open the FAQ Schema accordion in the editor.
  2. Click Add Question.
  3. Fill in question and answer.
  4. Repeat for each FAQ.
  5. Save.

Example

On a page about "solar panels" you add: "How much do solar panels cost?" with an answer. Google may show this question directly in search results.

Potential issues & solutions

  • FAQ not shown: Google decides whether to show FAQ accordions; ensure answers are objective and concise.
  • More than one FAQ block per page: keep it to one block to avoid schema errors.
