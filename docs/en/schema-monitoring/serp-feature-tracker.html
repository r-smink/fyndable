SERP Feature Tracker

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Shows which rich results (featured snippets, PAA, video carousels, local pack) you or your competitors appear in.

How to use it

  1. Go to SERP Feature Tracker.
  2. Enter a keyword.
  3. Click Track.
  4. Review which SERP features exist and who owns them.

Example

For "best solar panels" you see there's a featured snippet with a list. You optimize your content with a numbered list to claim that snippet.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Data is sometimes empty: SERP features change quickly; use the data as a trend, not as absolute.
