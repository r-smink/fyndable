SEO Report Export

Priority: low | Agency

What it does and what it gives you

Exports SEO reports to PDF, CSV or HTML. Useful for client reports or internal reviews.

How to use it

  1. Go to SEO Reports.
  2. Select a template or period.
  3. Click Generate.
  4. Download the report.

Example

At the end of the month you generate a PDF with rankings, site audit score and new content. You share it with your team or client.

Potential issues & solutions

  • PDF generation fails: check if your server has enough memory.
  • Report is empty: select a period where data was actually collected.
