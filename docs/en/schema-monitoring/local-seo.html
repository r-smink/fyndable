Local SEO

Priority: high | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Optimizes your site for local searches: business name, address, phone, opening hours and LocalBusiness schema.

How to use it

  1. Go to Settings → Local SEO.
  2. Fill in business name, address, phone number, email, opening hours and URLs.
  3. Choose your business type (e.g. LocalBusiness, Restaurant, Dentist).
  4. Save.
  5. Open a post and add Local SEO schema if needed.

Example

You have a dental practice in Rotterdam. Fyndable adds LocalBusiness schema with address, phone and opening hours, increasing your chances of appearing in the local pack.

Potential issues & solutions

  • NAP is inconsistent: ensure name, address and phone are exactly the same on every page.
  • Local pack still absent: this also requires Google Business Profile optimization; Fyndable helps on the site side.
