Dashboard Shell

Priority: low | All tiers

What it does and what it gives you

Fyndable replaces the standard WordPress admin with a full-page dashboard with a left sidebar. This gives you all SEO tools in one environment.

How to use it

  1. Click Fyndable in the WordPress menu to open the shell.
  2. Click through the menus in the left sidebar.
  3. Want to go back to standard WordPress? Click the × icon in the top right or open a page outside Fyndable.

Example

You're running a Site Audit and want to edit a post at the same time. Click ×, edit the post in WordPress, then reopen Fyndable.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Sidebar is empty: your license is not (or no longer) active, or your tier doesn't have access to that menu. Check Connection.
  • Page won't load in iframe: some WordPress pages refuse iframe loading due to X-Frame-Options. Open the page outside the shell.
