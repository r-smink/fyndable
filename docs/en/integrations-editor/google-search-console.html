Google Search Console

Priority: high | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Pulls performance data from GSC: queries, pages, CTR, positions and index status.

How to use it

  1. Go to Integrations → Google Search Console.
  2. Click Connect with Google.
  3. Choose the right property.
  4. Open Google Data → Search Console.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Property not visible: ensure your site is verified in GSC and you're logging in with an admin account.
  • Data is 2-3 days behind: this is normal for GSC.
