Unified SEO Meta-box

Priority: high | All tiers

What it does and what it gives you

An organized accordion panel with all SEO tools per post: SEO score, readability, smart tags, schema, social preview, canonical, hreflang and more.

How to use it

  1. Open a post in the editor.
  2. Scroll to the Fyndable SEO meta box (below the content).
  3. Expand the desired sections.
  4. Make changes and save the post.

Example

You open the meta box, expand Smart Tags, generate an SEO title, then expand Schema to add FAQ schema, and save.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Meta box not visible: check Screen Options at the top of the editor and check "Fyndable SEO".
  • Some accordions stay open: click the title to toggle; sometimes there's a JavaScript conflict with other plugins.
