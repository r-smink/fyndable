Post-level History

Priority: medium | Professional+

What it does and what it gives you

Shows per-post historical data: positions, clicks, impressions and CTR over time. Useful for measuring the impact of SEO changes.

How to use it

  1. Open a post in the editor.
  2. Expand Position Trends or History in the meta box.
  3. Review the chart with historical data.
  4. Compare with the date of your last change.

Example

You changed the title on March 1. In the chart you see CTR rising from 2% to 4% starting March 3.

Potential issues & solutions

  • No historical data: connect Search Console first in Integrations and wait a few days.
  • Data starts recently: Fyndable starts collecting data after connection; old data is not loaded retroactively.
