White-label

Priority: medium | Agency

What it does and what it gives you

Customize the login screen and WordPress menu with your own logo, company name and colors. Ideal for agencies reselling Fyndable.

How to use it

  1. Go to Settings → White Label.
  2. Fill in your company name.
  3. Upload a logo (URL or file).
  4. Fill in your primary and secondary colors (e.g. #379fd3 and #8f39ac).
  5. Save.
  6. Log out and back in to see the changes.

Example

You fill in: company name "Acme SEO", logo URL https://acme.com/logo.png, primary #ff6600. The login screen and Fyndable menu now show Acme SEO instead of Fyndable.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Logo not shown: check if the URL is directly accessible and the file is PNG or JPG.
  • Colors not applied: reload the page or clear browser cache.
  • White-label not visible: this feature requires Agency tier.
