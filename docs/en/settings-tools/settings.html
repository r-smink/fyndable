Settings

Priority: medium | All tiers

What it does and what it gives you

Central place for all plugin settings: general, post types, social, sitemap, breadcrumbs, webmaster tools, advanced and license.

How to use it

  1. Go to Settings.
  2. Choose a tab.
  3. Adjust settings.
  4. Click Save Changes.

Example

In Post Types you check product so WooCommerce products get SEO tools. In Social you fill in your Facebook and Twitter profiles.

Potential issues & solutions

  • Changes not saved: check if you have sufficient permissions (Administrator).
  • Tabs missing: some tabs are tier-dependent.
